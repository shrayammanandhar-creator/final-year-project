About NepWears

NepWears is an e-commerce store for traditional Nepali clothes, designed for Nepali people living abroad. Browse festival-ready outfits, cultural wear, and accessories by community, region, and occasion.

  • Tag-driven browsing by festival, event, region, caste, and style
  • Simple currency conversion from NPR to USD/EUR
  • Recommendations based on activity, wishlist, cart, and cultural tags
